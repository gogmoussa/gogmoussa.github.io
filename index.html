About & Engineering Ethos

Architecting Intelligent AI Systems & Modern Applications.

I'm George Moussa, a Software Engineer based in London, Ontario. I view software engineering through two complementary lenses: rigorous architectural precision and human-centered clarity. Whether developing an AST parser to eliminate architectural drift in large codebases or building local AI agents to automate system workflows without cloud latency, I emphasize privacy, performance, and maintainability across TypeScript, Python, and C#.
